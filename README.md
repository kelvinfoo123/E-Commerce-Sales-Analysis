# E-Commerce-Sales-Analysis
ExpressMart is an online E-commerce store based in India that sells consumer products such as electronics, books, fashion, sports products and groceries. ExpressMart started its operation in June 2024, and quickly expanded in 2025. In 1H 2025, ExpressMart reportedly experienced a 487% increase in revenue and 499% increase in orders. 

The Head of Sales has tasked the Data Analytics Division to analyse ExpressMart's sales performance in 1H 2026. The Sales Division aims to understand if there is an increase in sales compared to 1H 2025, and whether any sales strategy should be tweaked. 

## **Northstar Metrics** 
- Overall Sales Performance: To analyse sales performance, the key metrics that we would consider are sales revenue, number of orders placed and average order value (AOV).
- Product Sales Performance: We would also analyse sales performance across product categories and brands.
- Regional Sales Performance: We would also analyse regional sales performance across cities where orders were placed.
- Customer Profile Sales Performance: We would also analyse sales performance across customers of different age groups and membership level.

## **Analysis of Overall Sales Performance** 
- Total revenue, calculated using order value which is the product value before shipping and coupon discounts, remains constant. It experienced only a 0.07% decrease ($ 1,019,927) in total revenue compared to 1H 2025.
- Total orders follows a similar trend. Number of orders decreased slightly by 0.98% (589) compared to 1H 2025. This implies that the slight decrease in total revenue is linked to a decrease in orders.
- Despite the drop in orders, average order value (AOV) experienced a 0.92% increase ($219.42) compared to 1H 2025. This implies that customers are purchasing higher value items in each order.

## **Product Sales Performance** 
### **Revenue Analysis by Product** 
- The decrease in total revenue is primarily due to a decrease in revenue for groceries and sports product. Total revenue for groceries and sport products decreased by 3.42% ($346,186) and 3.72% ($4.4M) respectively.
- Total revenue for beauty, books and fashion product also decreased by 1.43% ($134,502), 0.75% ($33,130) and 1.05% ($852,655) respectively.
- Total revenue for electronics increased slightly by 0.16% ($1.6M) and total revenue for home products increased by 1.8% ($3.1M).
