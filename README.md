# E-Commerce-Sales-Analysis
ExpressMart is an online E-commerce store based in India that sells consumer products such as electronics, books, fashion, sports products and groceries. 

The Head of Sales has tasked the Data Analytics Division to analyse ExpressMart's sales performance in 1H 2026. The Sales Division aims to understand if there is an increase in sales compared to 1H 2025, and whether any sales strategy should be tweaked. 

## **Northstar Metrics** 
- Overall Sales Performance: To analyse sales performance, the key metrics that we would consider are sales revenue, number of orders placed and average order value (AOV).
- Product Sales Performance: We would also analyse sales performance across product categories and brands.
- Regional Sales Performance: We would also analyse regional sales performance across cities where orders were placed.
- Customer Profile Sales Performance: We would also analyse sales performance across customers of different age groups and membership level.
